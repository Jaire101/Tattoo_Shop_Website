Frequently asked questions

Before Your Appointment

Every project is different, but these answers cover the most common questions clients ask before booking.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on the size, detail, placement, artist, and time required. A final estimate is provided after the consultation.

Is a deposit required?

Yes. A nonrefundable deposit is required to reserve an appointment and begin custom design work.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. Reference material is encouraged, but the artist may adapt the design to improve composition, placement, and longevity.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Eat a full meal, stay hydrated, avoid alcohol, and wear comfortable clothing that provides access to the tattoo area.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Walk-ins may be accepted depending on artist availability, but consultations and scheduled appointments are recommended.
